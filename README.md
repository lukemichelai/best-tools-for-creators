Every tool here is one a working creator can open today. No dead products, no affiliate links, no paid placements.

**Legend:** `Free` costs nothing · `Freemium` usable free tier, paid plan for the rest · `Paid` subscription or one payment · `Open source` free and self hostable.

## Teleprompters

Reading a script while looking into the lens is the one thing that separates a take that feels natural from one that feels read. These are the apps that do it.

**[Teleprompter Premium+](https://www.teleprompterpremium.app):** a voice controlled teleprompter for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Instead of scrolling at a fixed speed and making you keep up, it listens and follows your natural pace. `Freemium` · iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779357842) · [Web editor](https://www.teleprompterpremium.app/create) · [Guides](https://guides.teleprompterpremium.app)

What the apps do, across every device:

* **Voice scrolling.** The script follows your speech, highlights each word as you say it, waits when you pause and picks up where you left off. No foot pedal and no assistant.
* **Record while it scrolls.** Built in camera recording with the prompter floating over the live preview, front or rear camera, with a countdown before the text starts moving.
* **Dual clocks.** Elapsed and remaining time computed live from your real reading pace, so you know before you start whether you land the sixty second cut.
* **Real rig support.** One tap horizontal or vertical mirroring for beam splitter glass, an adjustable cue marker, and full control of font, size, line spacing, margins, alignment and colours.
* **Ghost Mode on Mac.** The prompter floats semi transparent above every other app, so you can read from it during a Zoom call, a webinar or a live stream and nobody else sees it.
* **iPhone as the remote.** Pair the iPhone to the Mac prompter over the local network and drive play, pause, speed and jumps from your hand. Apple Watch does the same from your wrist, with the title and both clocks on screen.
* **Bring your own scripts.** Import PDF, Word, RTF, Markdown and plain text from Files, iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive, or write them in the app.
* **Share a script as a link.** Write in the browser on the web editor, send one link, and it opens straight inside the reader's app.
* **No account.** No sign up, no email, no password. Scripts live in your own iCloud, not on a server.

**[Speakflow](https://www.speakflow.com):** web prompter with voice following and shared team scripts. `Freemium` · Web
